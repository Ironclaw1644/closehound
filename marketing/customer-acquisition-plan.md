# CloseHound — Customer Acquisition Plan of Attack
_From $0 organic → paid. Last updated 2026-06-24. Also in Google Drive › closehound._

## What you're selling (frame everything around this)
CloseHound finds Section 8 houses where the government voucher rent **beats the mortgage** — the deals most investors never check for. The proof is real: a **$133,000 Cleveland house, voucher rent $1,925/mo, net cash flow +$480/mo, Deal Score 100**. Free tier = **100 credits (~10 markets), no card**; paid starts at **$39/mo**. The pitch: _"one deal pays for years."_

- **The ad** — 9:16 / 1:1 / 16:9 cuts + 3 A/B hook variants.
- **The Section 8 Playbook PDF** — your lead magnet (gate behind an email).
- **70 programmatic /markets SEO pages** on real HUD voucher data — free, compounding traffic.
- **A–F market grades** — a content goldmine ("best/worst Section 8 markets").
- **Seed audiences** — @walkperro (tech IG/TikTok) + @u_r_perfection (TikTok) to seed first views.
- **Tools you have** — Apollo (lead lists), Bright Data (social listening), an SEO toolkit.

## The funnel + success metrics (instrument FIRST)
Reach → Click → Free signup (100 credits) → Activation (runs first screen) → Aha (Score 90+ deal) → Paid ($39) → Retention.
- Early north-star: **activated free users/week** (people who run a screen). Then **free→paid %**.
- Milestones: 100 free signups → 10 paying → $1k MRR → first profitable paid channel.
- Before promoting anything: install free analytics (PostHog/Plausible) + track 4 events — `signup`, `first_screen`, `deal_open`, `upgrade`.

**ICP:** aspiring/small buy-and-hold Section 8 investors; out-of-state investors chasing Midwest cash flow (Cleveland/Memphis/Detroit/Birmingham/Indy); wholesalers; first-rental beginners.

## PHASE 1 — NO MONEY (weeks 1–8)
**Goal: prove the funnel converts + build a content habit.**

1. **Content engine (main motor) — 1 short video/day.** "Deal reveal" format: hook on a real number → show the app finding it → end on the Score.
   - Hooks (rotate the ad's 3): A "$1,925/mo to rent this $133k house." · B "rent that shows up guaranteed and beats your mortgage?" · C "most investors leave $5,700/yr on the table."
   - Series: "Section 8 deal of the day" · "best vs worst Section 8 markets" (A–F grades) · "why investors overpay" · myth reactions.
   - Every video: _"Screen your first market free — 100 credits, no card. Link in bio."_
   - Repurpose each into TikTok + Reels + Shorts + an X/BiggerPockets text post (1 idea = 4–5 posts). New @closehound handle; seed via @walkperro + @u_r_perfection.
2. **Community, value-first** (where your first 10 customers come from). Lead with data, disclose you built it.
   - BiggerPockets + Reddit: useful posts from your real data ("I analyzed 50 Cleveland ZIPs for Section 8 cash flow…"), soft-mention the tool. Answer questions daily.
   - Join 10–15 Section 8 / REI Facebook groups; share the Playbook when relevant.
   - Use **Bright Data social listening** to find people asking "what does Section 8 pay in [city]" → show up helpful.
3. **Data-PR play** (1 shareable/month): "The 25 best U.S. ZIP codes for Section 8 cash flow in 2026" → links back to /markets, earns SEO, pitch to REI newsletters.
4. **SEO** (compounding): index all 70 /markets pages in GSC; target "section 8 rent [city]", "fair market rent [zip]", "section 8 properties for sale [city]"; add 2–3 comparison posts.
5. **Founder-led outreach:** DM warm prospects on X/Reddit/BP; **Apollo** → 50–100 personalized cold emails offering a free deal report for THEIR market + the Playbook; partner with REI creators (free account + affiliate cut).
6. **Launch moments:** Product Hunt · "I built a free Section 8 deal screener" on BiggerPockets / r/realestateinvesting / Indie Hackers / Show HN.

**Exit criteria (don't spend money until ALL true):** organic free→paid conversions seen ✓ · Pixel/CAPI + analytics installed ✓ · 2–3 proven creative hooks ✓.

## PHASE 2 — WITH MONEY
**Goal: buy growth profitably once the funnel is proven.**

**The "kill ads in the first hour" system (not magic AI):** (1) Meta Pixel + Conversions API tracking Lead (signup) + Purchase (upgrade); (2) Meta **Automated Rules** — _"if after $X spend / N impressions CTR < 1% or cost-per-signup > $Y → pause"_; (3) good creative. That's the whole thing.

**Know your numbers first:** at $39/mo, max CAC ≈ LTV (`$39 × avg months × ~0.9 margin`). Keep CAC under that; aim payback < ~3 months. Track CPC, cost-per-signup, free→paid %, CAC, payback.

**Channels, in order:**
1. **Meta (IG/FB) conversion campaign** — main lever. 9:16 for Reels, 1:1 for feed, test all 3 hooks. Start **$20–50/day**, optimize Lead → switch to Purchase after ~50 conversions. Broad + interests (real estate investing, rental property, Section 8, BiggerPockets). Auto-kill losers; scale winners ~20–30%/day.
2. **Retargeting** (cheapest conversions): site visitors + free signups who didn't upgrade → "you left a deal on the table" + Playbook.
3. **Google Search ads** (bottom-funnel): "section 8 rent estimator", "fair market rent calculator", "section 8 properties for sale [city]".
4. **Influencer / affiliate** (often best ROI in REI): pay REI YouTubers/TikTokers; stand up a ~30% recurring affiliate program.

**Budget ladder:** $0 (Phase 1) → first **$300–500** test (Meta + retargeting) → scale winners by reinvesting a fixed % of revenue → add Search + influencers once Meta is stable.

## 30 / 60 / 90 day calendar
**Days 1–30 (foundation + organic):** analytics + events; submit /markets sitemap to GSC; stand up @closehound, pin the ad; 1 deal-reveal/day (seed via @walkperro + @u_r_perfection); join 15 communities, 2 value posts/week; Apollo 200-lead list + 50 emails. → 50–100 free signups.

**Days 31–60 (amplify + first sales):** double down on the 2–3 winning hooks; publish the "25 best ZIPs" piece; Product Hunt / Show HN; reach 5 REI creators; install Meta Pixel + CAPI now (warm it up). → first 5–10 paying customers.

**Days 61–90 (turn on paid):** $300–500 Meta test with auto kill-rules; retargeting for non-upgraders; small Google Search campaign; keep organic running. → one paid channel at/under target CAC, then scale with revenue.

## The one rule
**Spend your time (Phase 1) before you spend your money (Phase 2).** Organic proves the funnel and gives you the creative that makes paid work. When a $39 customer reliably costs less than they're worth — pour fuel on it.
